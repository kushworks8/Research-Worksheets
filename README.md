# 📚 Research Worksheets

A collection of research methodology worksheets, academic exercises, and study materials designed to strengthen understanding of research concepts and their practical applications.

## 📖 About

This repository contains worksheets and academic resources related to Research Methodology. It serves as a centralized collection of coursework, exercises, and learning materials for academic reference and revision.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand fundamental research methodology concepts.
- Explore different research approaches and techniques.
- Develop analytical and critical-thinking skills.
- Practice applying theoretical concepts through worksheets.
- Maintain organized academic resources for future reference.

## 📂 Repository Contents

The repository includes:
- Research methodology worksheets
- Academic assignments and exercises
- Research-related study materials
- Practical learning activities
- Supporting reference materials

## 🛠️ Usage

1. Browse the repository contents.
2. Open or download the required worksheet.
3. Use the materials for learning, practice, and academic reference.

## 🎓 Purpose

Created for academic learning, knowledge development, and maintaining an organized record of research methodology coursework.
